Hi I’m Bethany LaBelle — licensed massage therapist, certified health coach, and the heart behind Good Karma Collective.

I’ve been practicing massage therapy since 1999, and in that time I’ve had the privilege of working with thousands of clients — helping them recover, restore, and reconnect with how good their bodies can feel. I spent years building and running a thriving wellness practice, and I learned something important along the way: the most powerful thing you can offer someone isn’t just a great massage. It’s being truly seen and cared for.

Good Karma Collective is my fresh start — a space I’ve built from the ground up, with intention. A growing list of services and a vision for something bigger: a real wellness community rooted in warmth, trust, and results.

In addition to massage, I’m a certified Functional Medicine Health & Wellness Coach — which means I can support you not just on the table, but in the everyday habits and choices that shape how you feel long-term.

Whatever brings you here — I’m glad you found us.

Hi I’m Beverley Raymond — intuitive tarot reader, Reiki Master, and certified life coach, and the founder of Hummingbird Energy Healing.

People tend to call me “the Jane of all trades.” I’ve gathered a wide range of certifications over the years — everything from group fitness to crime and criminology — but they all trace back to one thing: I love helping people reconnect with themselves.

I’ve been working with Tarot since 2011 and reading publicly since 2019, and along the way I became a Reiki Master/Teacher and a Certified Life and Self Love Coach. My work is about shifting your focus, building self-awareness, and helping you rewrite the story that’s been holding you back — so you can step back into alignment and into your own power.

Whether we’re sitting with the cards, clearing energetic blocks, or simply talking something through, my hope is that you leave feeling seen, supported, and a little more like yourself.

When I’m not with clients, I’m teaching meditation, sharing what I’ve learned on podcasts, volunteering around the Northeast, or home with my son and my two pugs.

Whatever brought you here — I’d be honored to walk alongside you.
